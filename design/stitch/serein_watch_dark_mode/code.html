Eigenvectors and Eigenvalues: A Geometric Interpretation

3Blue1Brown · 24 min · 3 days ago

An exploration of what eigenvalues and eigenvectors actually represent geometrically in linear transformations, examining how invariant vectors determine the axes of stretch and scaling factors across multi-dimensional spaces. By examining how coordinate frames warp under matrix multiplication, we uncover why singular values emerge and how diagnostic matrix decompositions clarify structural stability across dynamic physical systems.
